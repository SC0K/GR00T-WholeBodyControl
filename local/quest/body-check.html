15-second body joint diagnostic

Records available joint poses, missing/estimated joints, and movement ranges inside this browser only. It sends no robot commands. Estimated joints are not proof of measured leg motion.

For the first 5 seconds stand still. For the next 5 seconds gently move one foot while keeping your hands still; use support if needed. For the last 5 seconds make a small comfortable crouch. The session closes automatically and shows the result on this page. No body data is uploaded. You can exit at any time.
